# Prove (hyperbolic_geometry)

To prove the theorem that \(1 + 1 = 2\), we can use the definition of the successor function in Peano arithmetic. According to Peano's axioms, the successor of a number \(n\) is defined as \(n + 1\). Therefore, the successor of 1 is \(1 + 1\). By definition, the successor of 1 is 2. Hence, we have:

\[
\begin{align*}
1 + 1 &= \text{successor of 1} \\
&= 2
\end{align*}
\]
