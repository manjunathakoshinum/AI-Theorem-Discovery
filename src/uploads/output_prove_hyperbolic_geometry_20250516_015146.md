# Prove (hyperbolic_geometry)

To prove that \(1 + 1 = 2\), we can use the following steps:

1. **Definition of Addition**: In standard arithmetic, the addition of two numbers is defined as the process of combining two quantities into a single quantity.

2. **Base Case**: We start with the definition of the number 1. In standard arithmetic, 1 is a unit quantity.

3. **Combining Units**: When we combine two units (1 and 1), we get a single quantity that is equivalent to the sum of the two units.

4. **Result**: By the definition of addition and the properties of unit quantities, we have \(1 + 1 = 2\).

Thus, the theorem \(1 + 1 = 2\) is proven.
